3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

After you complete a level, you can choose to watch a short rewarded ad for bonus points. Watching is never required to keep learning, and ads are never shown during tests.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). When an ad is requested, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID, and other device identifiers;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system, language, and app version;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • interactions with the ad, for example whether it was shown or completed;
  • diagnostic information.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, to prevent fraud, and, where you allow it, to personalise ads. To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Don't watch ads: simply tap “Continue without ad”. No ad is requested unless you choose to watch one.
  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you'll be asked for your choice before any ad is shown, and you can change it later.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. App permissions

The app uses only these Android permissions:

  • Internet and network state: to load optional ads.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services (attribution, topics): used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ads, subject to your settings and consent.
  • Wake lock: used by the ads SDK while an ad plays.

5. Security

All network connections made by the app use encrypted HTTPS. The app doesn't store your practice data in cloud backups.

6. Sharing and selling

We don't sell your personal information. We don't share information with anyone, other than the advertising data Google AdMob collects directly when you choose to watch an ad (section 3).

7. Keeping and deleting your information

  • Practice data: we don't keep it, because it never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app removes all of the app's data from your device.
  • Advertising data: Google keeps it under its own policies. You can control ad personalisation and your advertising ID in your device settings and your Google account.
